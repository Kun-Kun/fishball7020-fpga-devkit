WHY THESE 21 FILES AND NOT OTHERS
Everything else the FSBL needs comes from embeddedsw and was verified byte-identical
to it, so it does not belong in git. Measured against embeddedsw xilinx_v2022.2:

  FSBL app sources    23 of 27 identical  (ps7_init.{c,h} + ps7_parameters.xml come
                                           from the XSA; Xilinx.spec is generated)
  standalone BSP      89 of 89 identical
  18 drivers         117       identical, and exactly 15 differ - the 15 *_g.c
  xilffs               4       identical
                    ---------
                     229 files byte-identical

lscript.ld is NOT in this directory: it turned out to be identical to embeddedsw's
copy, i.e. upstream and static, not generated. That was worth checking rather than
assuming.

WHAT MAKES EACH ONE DESIGN-SPECIFIC
  xparameters.h   PS7 and PL addresses (XPAR_AXI_SPI_*, XPAR_FABRIC_*_INTR) AND six
                  software lines (FILE_SYSTEM_*) that xilffs's ffconf.h #ifdefs on.
                  Drift here is silent wrong behaviour, which is why the check below
                  exists.
  bspconfig.h     FPU_HARD_FLOAT_ABI_ENABLED, MICROBLAZE_PVR_NONE
  config/*_g.c    per-driver XConfig tables keyed on XPAR_<INSTANCE>_* NAMES, so
                  drift is a loud compile error
  inbyte/outbyte  emitted from the stdout peripheral (ps7_uart_1)
  Xilinx.spec     34 bytes; overrides gcc's *startfile to drop crt0%O%s, because
                  Xilinx's xil-crt0.o inside libxil.a provides startup instead
  config.make     3 lines of standalone build config

REGENERATING
These are only valid for ONE hardware design. If the block design changes, they must
be regenerated - today that still means a Vitis/xsct run:

    cd firmware/src/hdl/fsbl && xsct gen_fsbl_create.tcl
    # then copy out of system_top/ps7_cortexa9_0/standalone_domain/bsp/ps7_cortexa9_0/

and the XSA md5 above must be updated to match. See firmware/fsbl/README.md.
